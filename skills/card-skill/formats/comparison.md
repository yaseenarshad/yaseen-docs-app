# Comparison

Use it when the learner must tell two or more related things apart. The difference is the point, so keep them on one card. **Title: hidden.** Name both things on one line: "A vs B". Hide it with `--hide-title`, not with `{{ }}` (see [[Titles]]).

## Skeleton

- Title: "A vs B". Hide it on the front.
- Front, part 1: one situation that shows each thing, each one hidden.
- Front, part 2: one definition line for each, side by side, each term hidden.
- Back: the terms visible, and what sets them apart hidden.
- Do not invent a sharp line between meanings that overlap.

## Example

```markdown
Weather vs climate

A rainy week in July tells you about the {{weather}}. Forty years of rainfall records tell you about the {{climate}}.

The {{weather}} is the state of the air over hours or days.
The {{climate}} is the usual pattern of weather over many years.
---
Weather is {{short term}}: today's rain, wind and heat.
Climate is {{long term}}: the average and the range over decades.
```
