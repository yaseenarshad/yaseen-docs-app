# Person

Use it to recall who a person is and what they did that matters. A quote by them uses [[quote]]. An idea they made uses [[concept]]. **Title: hidden.** The title is the name, and the name is the answer.

## Skeleton

- Title: the full name, hidden.
- Front: a question with one or two clues that point to this person only. A job title alone is not enough.
- Back: a short line on who they were, then "Why remember" with a few hidden points.
- Do not show the name again anywhere on the card. Use "she", "he" or "they".

## Example

```markdown
{{Marie Curie}}

Which scientist discovered radium and was the first person to win two Nobel Prizes?
---
A Polish-born physicist and chemist (1867 to 1934) who worked in Paris.

***

WHY REMEMBER HER

- She coined the word {{radioactivity}}.
- She named the element {{polonium}} after her home country.
```
