# Procedure

Use it to recall an action or the steps of a task. **Title: shown.** The title names the task. The steps are the answer.

## Skeleton

- Title: the task, in a few words.
- Front: the situation, and the question of what to do.
- Back: the steps that matter, hidden. A note on what to watch for.
- For a long procedure, make one card for each stage or decision. Keep the source's order.

## Example

```markdown
Force quit a frozen app on a Mac

An app on a Mac stops responding and will not close. What do you do?
---
Press {{Option + Command + Esc}}, choose the app in the list, then click {{Force Quit}}.

Any unsaved work in that app is lost.
```
