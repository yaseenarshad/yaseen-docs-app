# Fact

Use it for an organization, a date, a place or another single fact. A person uses [[person]]; a book uses [[book]]. **Title: hidden** when the title would give the answer away. Shown when it is only a cue.

## Skeleton

- Title: the name being learned.
- Front: a short question, then a sentence that teaches the link, with the answer hidden.
- Back: a few lines on why it matters.
- For a date, keep the event visible and hide the date. Date a fact that can change: "As of 2026".

## Example

```markdown
{{World Health Organization}}

Which United Nations agency declared COVID-19 a pandemic in March 2020?

The {{World Health Organization}} leads the United Nations' work on public health.
---
Founded in 1948. Its headquarters are in Geneva.
```
