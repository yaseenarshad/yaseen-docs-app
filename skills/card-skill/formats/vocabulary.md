# Vocabulary

Use it to learn a word: what it means and how to use it. **Title: hidden.** The title is the word, and the word is the answer. Hide it with `--hide-title`, not with `{{ }}` (see [[Titles]]).

## Skeleton

- Title: the word. Hide it on the front.
- Front, part 1: a natural sentence that uses the word. Someone who does not know the word still gets the point.
- Front, part 2: a definition as a sentence, with the meaning visible and the word hidden.
- Back: the part of speech, then the word visible and its meaning hidden.
- Back, after `***`: examples. Pronunciation, origin or a nearby word only when they add something.

## Example

```markdown
Terse

I asked how the meeting went, and his reply was {{terse}}: "Fine." Nothing more.

A reply that uses very few words, and can seem unfriendly, is {{terse}}.
---
Adjective.

Terse means {{brief to the point of seeming unfriendly}}.

***

EXAMPLES

- Her {{terse}} email said only "No."
- The manual is so {{terse}} that new users get lost.
```
