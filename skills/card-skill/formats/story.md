# Story

Use it to recall a story and tell it in your own words. A story has people, and something happens to them. An argument or a worked example is not a story. **Title: hidden.** The title is `Story: <Person> - <cue>`. Hide it with `--hide-title`, not with `{{ }}` (see [[Titles]]).

## Skeleton

- Front: a situation in a conversation where the story would help, and a short hint.
- Back: the story in short blocks, each starting with a label: WANT, OBSTACLE, CHANGE, TAKEAWAY. Use only the labels the story needs.
- Hide only a few key words. The goal is to retell it, not to recite it.
- Say where the story comes from, and whether it is certain.

## Example

````markdown
```card
A friend says their best ideas come when they stop working on the problem. Which story fits, about a gold crown?
---
**WANT:** A king wants to know if his new crown is pure gold. He asks Archimedes to find out without damaging it.

**OBSTACLE:** Archimedes knows its weight, but he cannot measure the volume of such an odd shape.

**CHANGE:** He steps into a full bath and sees the water spill over. The water a body pushes out {{equals its own volume}}. He runs into the street, shouting "{{Eureka}}", "I have found it."

**TAKEAWAY:** A hard problem can give way when you step back from it.

***

SOURCE: the Roman writer Vitruvius told it about two hundred years later. It may be a legend.
```
````
