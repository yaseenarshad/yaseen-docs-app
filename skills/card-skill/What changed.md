# What changed

## 2026-11-14: Every card's title is on its card line

- A card's title is on its card line, right after its ID: `<!-- card: DEMO-41, title "Longest river" -->`. This is the same for a card inside a page and for a card file.
- A card's text is only its sides. Its first line is part of the question, never its title.
- A card file's front matter holds only `kind: card`. It has no `title:`.
- `card add --title` and `card new --title` write the title on the card line. `--hide-title` adds `notitle` after it.
- A card you write by hand gets the line `<!-- card: title "<title>" -->` below its closing fence. The app adds the ID.
- The app moved the titles of your cards one time. [[Start here]], [[Titles]] and every format's example show the new shape.
