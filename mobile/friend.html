A friend sent you a Notecard link

Notecard is the phone app of Yaseen Docs. You review your cards with it. Your friends see your review numbers. They never see your cards.

Copy this code. Open the app. Tap Paste.

  1. Tap Copy.
  2. Open Notecard from your Home Screen.
  3. In Friends, tap Paste a friend's link.

This link has no friend code. Ask your friend to send the link again.

No Notecard on this phone yet?

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  2. Open Notecard from your Home Screen. Connect your vault. The app shows the steps.
  3. After you connect your vault, tap the link in Messages again, and tap Copy.
  4. In Friends, tap Paste a friend's link.

Safari and the Home Screen app do not share their data. Use the app on the Home Screen, not this page.
